# The AI Ecosystem

An interactive map of how AI gets built, from sand and electricity to the chatbot on your phone.

**Live page:** https://minervakhajuria-svg.github.io/TheAINetwork/

## What it shows

The AI industry runs on a physical supply chain. This map lays it out as a connected network of 19 parts, grouped into seven layers:

- **Semiconductor Supply Chain:** materials, chip design tools, fab equipment
- **Chips & Hardware:** chip designers, foundries, memory, packaging, networking, servers
- **Physical Infrastructure:** energy, power and cooling, data center builders
- **Compute Providers:** hyperscalers and GPU clouds
- **Models & Data:** model labs, training data, AI tooling
- **Applications & Demand:** the apps and users where value is realised
- **Capital:** the money that funds it all

## How to use it

- **Click any node** to zoom in and see what flows into it, what it supplies, and the key companies in that part of the network.
- **Click a company** to visit its website.
- **Search** (top right, or press `/`) for a company or part of the network to jump straight to it.
- **Drag** to pan, **scroll** to zoom, and press **Esc** to return to the full view.

## About

The whole map is a single self-contained `index.html` file with no dependencies. Company logos load from Google's favicon service.

Key players are illustrative, not exhaustive, and reflect the market as of 2026. The industry moves quickly, so suggestions and corrections are welcome.
